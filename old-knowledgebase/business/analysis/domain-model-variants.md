**Why (rigorous under constraints + max skepticism):**
- **Maintainability (clear boundaries):** 5 high-level (per 01 goal "deliberately keep few") with internal crisp aggregates (Quest + VersionSnapshot global in Content as single source; Grant as capability AR; PlayerQuestProgress or equivalent scoped AR for all per-player progress on a quest (addresses "Play & Progress" god + multi-device/merge); Ledger separate for coins).
